The 30-day plan

Exercises take about 10 minutes each and can be practised in a quiet moment or a pre-shift huddle.

Before launchSuccess plan

The manager names one priority store problem, agrees the measures and records a baseline. Output: a one-page success plan.

Week 1Understand and build trust

Discovery, listening and confirming needs. Output: a scored discovery role-play.

Week 2Assess and validate

Appropriate assessment, clear explanations, and try, walk and compare. Output: an observed fitting checklist.

Week 3Recommend and guide

Relevant recommendations, transparent price conversations and respectful handling of concerns. Output: an assessed customer-decision scenario.

Week 4Bring it together

A complete customer interaction, reassessment and manager review. Output: a closing scorecard and a 90-day improvement plan.

If the baseline shows one clear weakness, the manager can give that week extra coaching time without dropping the rest of the journey.

How progress is measured

We keep three things separate, so learning, skill and store results are never confused.

1. Learning

Completion

Assigned lessons and coaching exercises completed by each learner.

2. Practice

Manager observations

Checklists completed by the manager while watching each skill in practice.

3. Store

Weekly scorecard

Scans, insole transactions linked to scans, scan-to-insole conversion, insole gross profit, returns and coaching completion, in your chosen currency.

Scan-to-insole conversion = insole transactions linked to scans ÷ scans × 100. When a week has no scans, conversion is shown as “not applicable” rather than zero.

Each week also records context such as stockouts, promotions and staffing changes. Before-and-after figures show what changed during the pilot, not what caused it. Seasonality, stock and staffing can all move the numbers.

Store results never decide whether someone earns a BrightPath credential. Credentials depend only on assessed knowledge and practical skill.

Questions

Does the pilot renew or turn into a subscription?

No. The founding pilot is a single US$200 payment for 30 days. Nothing renews automatically. At the end you can choose to stop, or to continue with Academy Learn or Enterprise.

When do we pay?

Apply first. If your store is a good fit, we confirm a start date and send a secure Stripe payment link. Your place is confirmed once payment is received.

What happens to learners’ access after 30 days?

Pilot access ends after the 30 days. Learners keep any badges they earned. You can continue on a membership or Enterprise plan if you want ongoing access.

Will our store data be shared?

No. Your scorecard figures are visible only to your store’s managers and BrightPath. Any examples we reuse for training are anonymised and only used with your permission.

We do not have a scanner. Can we join?

This pilot is designed around scan conversations. Teams without a scanner can use Academy Learn, which covers hands-on fitting, and can ask us about Enterprise coaching.
