Refund policy

Effective 28 October 2026

We want you to be confident buying from BrightPath Academy. Here is how refunds and cancellations work.

One-time programme purchases

  • 14-day guarantee: if you are not satisfied, ask for a refund within 14 days of purchase and we will refund you in full, provided you have completed less than 25% of the programme.
  • After 14 days, or once more than 25% is complete, purchases are not refundable except where required by law.

Memberships and Enterprise seats

  • Cancel at any time by emailing us. Your access continues until the end of the month or year you have paid for, and you will not be charged again.
  • New annual memberships can be refunded in full within 14 days of the first payment if less than 25% of any programme has been completed.
  • We do not give partial refunds for unused time in a monthly or annual period, except where required by law.
  • Enterprise agreements signed separately follow the refund terms in that agreement.

Your consumer rights

Our services come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. If a programme has a major problem, you are entitled to a refund or other remedy regardless of this policy.

How to request a refund

Contact us at globalbrightpath1@gmail.com or WhatsApp +61 485 502 264 with your name, the email used to pay and what you bought. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe, usually within 5 to 10 business days depending on your bank. Access to refunded programmes is removed.
