Privacy policy

Effective 28 October 2026

We collect only what we need to run the Academy, and we never sell your personal information.

1. Who we are

BrightPath Academy is part of BrightPath Global, Gold Coast, Queensland, Australia. We aim to handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles. Contact us about privacy at globalbrightpath1@gmail.com or WhatsApp +61 485 502 264.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, organisation, role, learning pathway and a securely encrypted password.
  • Learning records: lessons completed, quiz and exam results, badges and certificates.
  • Purchases: your name, email, what you bought and payment status. Card details are handled by Stripe and never reach us.
  • Enquiries: when you apply, download a brochure, request an advisor or a team proposal, we collect your name, email, mobile or WhatsApp number, country, organisation, role, interest, team size and any message.
  • Feedback: your name, optional email, role, organisation, rating, comment and whether we may publish it.
  • Technical data: your browser stores your progress and sign-in so the Academy works. Our hosting providers keep standard logs such as IP address and browser type for security.

3. How we use it

  • To provide your programmes, track progress and issue and verify certificates.
  • To process payments and manage memberships and team seats.
  • To reply to enquiries and follow up by phone, WhatsApp or email when you have asked us to.
  • To improve our programmes and, with your permission, publish testimonials.
  • To keep the service secure and meet legal obligations.

4. Who we share it with

We use trusted providers to run the Academy: Supabase (accounts and database), Netlify (website hosting and forms), Stripe (payments), Google (email and website fonts) and jsDelivr (website code delivery). Some of these providers store data outside Australia, including in the United States. If your organisation pays for your seat, we may share your progress, results and certificates with that organisation. We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising.

5. Cookies and tracking

We use only essential browser storage to keep you signed in and save your progress. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

6. How long we keep it

We keep account and certificate records while your account is active and for up to 7 years afterwards so certificates can still be verified and to meet tax and legal requirements. Enquiries we do not convert are deleted within 2 years. You can ask us to delete your information sooner.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it.
  • Ask us to delete your account and data, subject to legal record-keeping.
  • Tell us to stop contacting you about our programmes at any time.

Email us and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

8. Security

Accounts are protected by encrypted passwords and access rules so each learner can only see their own records. No online system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

9. Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version here with its effective date.
